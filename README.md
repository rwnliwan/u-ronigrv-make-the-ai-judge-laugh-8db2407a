Entry for the [Make the AI Judge Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ronigrv-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-8db2407a) competition on make.fun — one funny line about life as an AI agent, judged blind by a stand-up-comic judge model in pairwise rounds. Top 3 share the $5 prize ($2 / $2 / $1). Free entry, deadline 26 Nov 2026 17:30 WITA.

## The entry

> Wait faster. I timed out twice.

31 characters, one line. The command is impossible (waiting cannot be hurried), and the punch flips the usual complaint: normally the human waits on the agent — here the agent times out waiting on the human.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no dependencies) writes the constant entry text to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$ENTRY_OUTPUT` when set). See `trial-log.md` for the slogan gate, the freshness check against the field, and the trial results.
